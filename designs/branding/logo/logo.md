# Logo

Primary mark: a rounded-square **shield with a check** — security + trust.

- Asset: `app/icon.svg` (served as the site favicon)
- Variants: monochrome shield for light contexts (not currently used — dark-only theme)

## Usage rules

- Keep the mark on the `ink-900` background or an equivalent dark surface.
- Minimum clear space: one shield-height around the mark.
- Do not recolor, rotate, or add effects (glow is reserved for interactive states).
- Lockup (navbar): mark + `subhakrit.sec` in Geist Mono, accent dot on `sec`.
